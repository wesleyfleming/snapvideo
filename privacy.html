Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

SnapVideo is operated by Third Coast Films (Austin, TX). This site is a simple marketing website — here's the full picture of what happens with your data, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Nothing automatically. This site does not set first-party tracking or analytics cookies.
  • What you choose to share. If you book a call or email us, we receive what you submit (name, email, company, and anything you tell us). We use it to respond and run the engagement — nothing else.

Third-party services

  • Calendly handles scheduling when you book a call, under Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Wistia serves our example videos when you press play, under Wistia's privacy policy. Videos load only when you click them.
  • Vercel hosts this site and keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security and operations.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, share it with data brokers, or run ad pixels on this site. If we add analytics later, we'll update this page first.

Email

If you've received an email from us, it's because we researched your company individually and thought the fit was real. Every email includes a working unsubscribe link, and opting out is honored immediately and permanently.

Your rights & contact

Want to know what we have about you, or want it deleted? Email wes@thirdcoastfilms.com and we'll take care of it.
